Processing

Resting correction: Sampling − Resting within the same cycle → average Cycle 2–6 per replicate → Z-score standardization across 8 sensors → PCA. Sensors: G2, G3, G5, G6, G10, G11, G13, G14. Calibration curve: multiple linear regression of the five known blend proportions against PC1–PC3 scores.

Exploratory calibration: 2 replicates per composition. The mean view shows the centroid of the two replicate scores from the same fitted PCA model, not additional independent samples. Validate the calibration with more independent blend samples before using it for quantitative prediction.
