Processing protocol

For each sensor and cycle: mean Sampling over the full Sampling interval minus mean Resting in the same cycle. Average corrected response across Cycles 2–6 for each replicate, then average Rep1 and Rep2 within each blend ratio.

No Blank subtraction. No Unknown samples. Calibration contains only five blend-ratio means. Pure samples are not included in this calibration page.
