Processing notes

Within each cycle and sensor: Response = mean(Sampling) − mean(Resting). Cycle responses were averaged over Cycles 2–6 to obtain one feature vector per replicate. StandardScaler was fitted on calibration only; PCA was fitted on standardized calibration responses, and unknown responses were transformed using the same scaler and PCA.

Calibration date: 10–11 Nov 2026 · Unknown date: 12 Nov 2026 · Instrument/day effects may influence cross-day predictions.
